# Move-Abroad Pitch: Mexico vs Iceland. Guiones individuales

**¿Qué piden "las frases"?** Cada integrante escribe **su propio guion de 6 a 8 oraciones** sobre **su tema** y lo sube a Classroom. Ese guion debe tener:

| Requisito | Mínimo |
|---|---|
| Comparativos (*cheaper than, more expensive than, higher, better…*) | 2 |
| Superlativo (*the safest, the most important, the best…*) | 1 |
| Modificadores (*much, a lot, significantly, a little, slightly, a bit*) | 2 |
| Una razón con **"The main reason is…"** | 1 |
| Palabras de la Lesson A (*income, afford, standard of living, basic, possession, financial, leisure, division*) | 4 |

Además, cada persona debe hablar **al menos 1 minuto** (el pitch completo dura 4 minutos). Los números del guion deben ser **los mismos que los de la gráfica**.

Reparto del equipo (coincide con la diapositiva 2):

- **Areli:** abre con el destino (diapositiva 1) y presenta *Housing & Food*.
- **Elizabeth:** presenta *Transport & Safety*.
- **Juan Pablo:** presenta *Salary & Leisure* y cierra con la recomendación (diapositiva 3).

En cada guion, **negritas** = comparativos/superlativos, *cursivas* = modificadores y las palabras de la Lesson A van subrayadas con `código`.

---

## 1. Areli: Destination, Housing & Food (diapositivas 1 y 2)

1. Hello, everyone! Our team wants to move to Iceland, a beautiful Nordic island, and today we are going to compare it with Mexico.
2. I am going to talk about housing and food, which are the **most basic** expenses for any family (`basic`).
3. In Mexico, the rent for a one-bedroom apartment in the city centre is about 600 dollars a month, but in Iceland it is 2,100 dollars, so it is *much* **more expensive**.
4. Food is also *significantly* **more expensive**: a meal in an inexpensive restaurant costs 10 dollars in Mexico and 28 dollars in Iceland.
5. It is more expensive, but people in Iceland have a **higher** `income`, so they can `afford` these prices.
6. The main reason is that rent takes less than half of an Icelandic salary, but almost all of a Mexican salary.
7. For this reason, I think Iceland offers a **better** `standard of living` for families.
8. Now, my teammate Elizabeth will tell you about transport and safety.

✔ Comparativos: more expensive, higher, better · Superlativo: the most basic · Modificadores: much, significantly · Lesson A: basic, income, afford, standard of living

---

## 2. Elizabeth: Transport & Safety (diapositiva 2)

1. Thank you, Areli. I am going to talk about transport and safety.
2. A monthly public transport pass costs about 25 dollars in Mexico, but in Iceland it costs 90 dollars, so transport is *a lot* **more expensive**.
3. It is more expensive, but workers in Iceland can easily `afford` it with their `income`.
4. Also, Iceland has only about 390,000 people, so its cities are *much* **smaller** and **less crowded** than Mexican cities.
5. However, **the biggest** difference between the two countries is safety, which is `basic` for a good `standard of living`.
6. According to the Global Peace Index, Iceland is **the safest** country in the world, number one, while Mexico is number 135.
7. The main reason is that Iceland has very little crime, so people can walk at night and leave their `possessions` without fear.
8. Now, Juan Pablo will tell you about jobs, salaries and our final recommendation.

✔ Comparativos: more expensive, smaller, less crowded · Superlativos: the biggest, the safest · Modificadores: a lot, much · Lesson A: afford, income, basic, standard of living, possessions

---

## 3. Juan Pablo: Salary, Leisure & Recommendation (diapositivas 2 y 3)

1. Thank you, Elizabeth. I am going to talk about jobs, salaries and leisure.
2. The average salary in Mexico is about 700 dollars a month, but in Iceland it is 4,800 dollars, so it is almost seven times **higher**.
3. This means that people in Iceland have a *much* **better** `financial` situation and a **higher** `income`.
4. `Leisure` is also *significantly* **more expensive**: a cinema ticket costs 5 dollars in Mexico and 17 dollars in Iceland.
5. If we look at the chart, prices in Iceland are about three times higher, but salaries are almost seven times higher, so people can `afford` *a lot* more.
6. The most important difference is rent: it takes 86 percent of a Mexican salary, but only 44 percent of an Icelandic salary.
7. The main reason we recommend Iceland is that it offers a **higher** `standard of living` and **the best** safety in the world.
8. In conclusion, Iceland is **colder** and **more expensive** than Mexico, but it is a **better** place to live, so our recommendation is: move to Iceland, with a plan! Thank you!

✔ Comparativos: higher, better, more expensive, colder · Superlativos: the best, the most important · Modificadores: much, significantly, a lot · Lesson A: financial, income, leisure, afford, standard of living

---

### Consejos para el pitch (criterios A y E)

- **No leas:** usa la diapositiva como guía. Cada uno tiene su nombre en la gráfica.
- **Pasa el turno** con la última frase de tu guion ("Now, my teammate… will tell you about…").
- **Señala la gráfica** cuando digas un número. Juan Pablo puede señalar los anillos (86% vs 44%) en la diapositiva 3.
- Para llegar a 1 minuto, habla despacio y haz una pausa después de cada número.
- Si te equivocas, corrígete con naturalidad ("Sorry, I mean…"), porque el criterio E valora la autocorrección.
