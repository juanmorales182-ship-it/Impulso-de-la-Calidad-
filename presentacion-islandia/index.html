Our recommendation

Move to Iceland, with a plan.

Iceland is more expensive than Mexico, but salaries are much higher, so people can afford a better standard of living. It is also the safest country in the world.

The most important difference

How much of the average salary goes to rent?

86%
Mexico
44%
Iceland

1-bedroom rent in the city centre ÷ average monthly salary: $600 ÷ $700 in Mexico; $2,100 ÷ $4,800 in Iceland.

Why yes

A much higher income, the best safety in the world, free public healthcare and incredible nature.

Be careful

Basic things cost about three times more, and winters are long, dark and a lot colder than in Mexico.

Best for

People with a job offer and savings for the first months, who love nature more than sunny beaches.
